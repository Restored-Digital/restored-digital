Get in touch What I do

Sound familiar?

  • Your business email is a Gmail address, and some of your messages may be landing in spam.
  • You own a domain but nobody has set it up, or you're not sure where it's registered.
  • Your website is down, and nobody knows who was supposed to be looking after it.

That's usually a setup problem, not a design problem, and it's fixable.

What I do

Setup

  • Domain registered in your name
  • Business email on your own domain, configured so your messages reach inboxes
  • A simple, fast website on Cloudflare
  • Google Business Profile and a review link
  • A contact form tested end to end

Monthly care (optional)

  • Uptime and security monitoring
  • Domain, DNS, and SSL kept current
  • Small content edits
  • A short check-in each quarter

Rescue

For a site that's down or a domain nobody manages. I find out what's wrong, then fix it or move you somewhere that stays up. Diagnosis first, quote second.

Ownership. Your domain, hosting account, and email accounts are created in your name from day one. I'm added as an administrator, never the owner.

Scope. Anything outside this, like custom software or complex IT systems, I'll tell you plainly and point you to someone who does it.

Pricing. I quote after a short conversation, in writing, before any work starts.
